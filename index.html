Important Health Notice

CardioShield-AgeLess is an educational screening prototype, not a medical diagnostic tool. Its output is not a clinically validated estimate of an individual's probability of a heart attack and must not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Never delay urgent care because of a model result. If you experience possible heart attack symptoms, such as chest pain or severe breathlessness, seek emergency medical care immediately.
